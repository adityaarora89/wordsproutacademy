Level 1 · Beginner

Seedlings

A gentle first step into reading. Children discover that every letter makes a sound, and that sounds join together to make words.

LevelBeginner
Classes / week3
Total sessions30
FormatGroup or 1:1

What your child will learn

  • The sound each of the 26 letters makes
  • Recognising and naming lowercase letters
  • Joining sounds to read short 2–3 letter words like cat, sun and map
  • A first set of sight words to grow early vocabulary

By the end of the course

  • Hears and plays with the individual sounds in words (phonemic awareness)
  • Reads simple words on their own
  • Has a strong foundation for moving on to Sprouts

How classes work: short, playful sessions built around songs, games and stories, so children stay engaged while learning the basics.

Level 2 · Intermediate

Sprouts

Building on letter sounds, this level turns early readers into independent ones, moving from single words to full sentences and first storybooks.

LevelIntermediate
Classes / week3
Total sessions66
FormatGroup or 1:1

What your child will learn

  • About 42 phonic sounds, including letter combinations and common spelling patterns
  • Blending sounds to read and segmenting words to spell
  • Reading and writing words of up to 8 letters
  • Building vocabulary and putting words together into sentences
  • Tricky words and high-frequency sight words

By the end of the course

  • Reads and writes words of up to 8 letters with confidence
  • Forms simple sentences easily
  • Reads age-appropriate storybooks independently
  • Enjoys reading and wants to keep going

How classes work: interactive lessons that combine reading, sentence building and writing, so each skill reinforces the others.

Level 3 · Advanced

Storytellers

For confident readers ready for the next challenge: longer words, accurate spelling, and understanding and talking about the stories they read.

LevelAdvanced
Classes / week3
Total sessions60
FormatGroup or 1:1

What your child will learn

  • Advanced phonics for reading words of up to 10 letters
  • Spelling 8–10 letter words independently
  • Clear pronunciation of words and sentences
  • Reading storybooks with confidence and understanding
  • Talking about stories and expressing ideas creatively

By the end of the course

  • Reads and spells long words confidently
  • Speaks more clearly and understands more of what they read
  • Picks up age-appropriate books and reads them independently
  • Shares ideas through their own storytelling

How classes work: reading is paired with discussion, so children practise explaining what happened in a story and why.

For parents

Good to know

How much do parents need to help?

Very little. Classes are designed so children learn on their own; a little encouragement to keep them focused is all that's needed.

What if my child misses a class?

To protect children's privacy, classes are not recorded. A make-up class can be arranged, depending on availability.

Can a session be rescheduled?

Yes, one session per course can be moved to another time, subject to availability.

Is there practice material for home?

Yes. Printable worksheets are shared so your child can practise between classes.

Is my child's information safe?

Yes. Personal details are handled under strict data-protection practices.

Are there any extra costs?

No. All lessons and learning materials are included in the course.
